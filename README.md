# Red-Vector---Steam-Game

A retro corridor shooter. Seven sectors. One vector. Move through tight, ray casted halls, clear every target, and pick up the next weapon as you go. Pistol first. Shotgun and knife later. A final warden waits in Sector 7. When the campaign ends, Endless Mode keeps the corridors open. Every tenth sector is a boss. Armor, ammo, health and weapons scale as you survive. Clear the corridors. Beat the Game.
